    01.5Trade options

    #PricePWV

    Every idea is projected the same way and sized by the portfolio and rules in tab 02. Reward : risk is the PWV's gain over the loss at the stop-working price. Click a row (or its symbol) to chart it; click a heading to sort by it.

    The benchmark found that 80% ranges drawn from price history held the outcome only 70–77% of the time, so treat these bands as the memo's view, not a guarantee. Example figures, not a real company.
